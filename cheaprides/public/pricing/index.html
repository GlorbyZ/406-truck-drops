Questions

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Paid alerts are $7 per month or $49 per year. Your card is collected up front. When the trial ends, the plan auto-bills that card unless you canceled before then.

How do I cancel?

Cancel anytime from the account page. That opens the Stripe billing portal. Access continues until the period ends, including a trial that is still running.

Do you refund a partial month or year?

We do not refund partial periods, unless a refund is required by law.

What categories can I follow?

Beater commuters, winter beaters, first cars, mechanic's specials, and fun and cheap. An empty list means every deal.

How late is the free feed?

The free tier is a 24-hour delayed digest. Paid members see a new listing or a price drop right away.

What area do you cover?

Billings, Montana and about 150 miles around it, including Laurel, Lockwood, Columbus, and Red Lodge.

Where do the cars come from?

Listings come from public third-party marketplaces. We do not guarantee accuracy or availability. 406 Cheap Rides is not a party to any vehicle sale.
