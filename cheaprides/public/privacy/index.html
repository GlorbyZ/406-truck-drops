Privacy

Privacy policy

This policy explains what 406TruckDrops collects to run 406 Cheap Rides. Questions go to support@406truckdrops.com.

Data we collect

  • Your email address, so we can sign you in and send alerts.
  • An optional phone number, if you later add one for text alerts.
  • Your Stripe customer id, so we can tie the account to billing.
  • Category preferences you choose for which deals to hear about.
  • Plan, status, and the date your trial or paid period ends.

Who handles payments and email

Payments are handled by Stripe. We do not store your card number. Email is handled by Resend. Those companies process the data they need to charge the card or deliver the message.

We do not sell data

We do not sell your data. We use it to run the alerts, the account, and billing, and to fix problems with the service.

Unsubscribe

Every alert email includes an unsubscribe link. The confirm email does too. Unsubscribing stops alert email. It does not cancel a paid plan. Cancel billing from the account page.
